Farmyard flying game · 2 to 4 players

LoopingLouie

Powered by Aquigai

Louie is flying circles over the farm, and whenever he swoops low he knocks a chicken off its perch. Flip your lever as he passes to send him looping away. The last farmer with a chicken left wins.

Mode
Your leverPress your key (Q, P, M or Z) or tap your corner of the screen as Louie reaches it.
Short or longTap for a little hop. Hold a moment and he dives onto your neighbour's chickens; hold longer and he loops over them onto the next farmer. Hold too long and he goes all the way up.
ChickensEvery farmer starts with three. Lose all three and you are out of the game. Louie flies faster every 40 seconds.
